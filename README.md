# Skyline HQ

Build a premium, modern, visually impressive and production-quality HOME PAGE for my company:

SKYLINE BUILDING MATERIAL TRADING

We are a building materials trading company supplying electrical products, building materials, construction-related products and other related products to contractors, construction companies, developers, maintenance companies, businesses and customers.

For design and product/category inspiration, use this website as a reference:

https://www.danitrading.ae/

Review the reference website where possible to understand the type of brands, product categories and building/electrical products that a company in this industry offers.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT copy their website design.

Do NOT copy their exact layout, branding, text or visual identity.

Create an original and significantly more modern website for Skyline Building Material Trading.

Use the reference primarily for understanding the industry, product categories, brands and product types.

If information from the reference website cannot be verified, use editable placeholders rather than inventing specific facts.

1. MAIN DESIGN CONCEPT

The website should look like a premium UAE-based building materials and electrical trading company.

The visual identity must be based primarily on:

BLUE + YELLOW

Use blue as the dominant corporate color and yellow as the strong accent/CTA color.

Suggested visual direction:

Deep/royal blue for primary branding

Bright yellow for CTA buttons, highlights and important accents

White/light neutral backgrounds

Dark charcoal for text

Very subtle blue gradients where appropriate

The combination should feel:

Professional + Strong + Modern + Industrial + Reliable

Do NOT make the website look childish or overly colorful.

Yellow should be used strategically for:

CTA buttons

Small highlights

Icons

Hover states

Important statistics

Decorative lines

Product/category accents

Blue should remain the primary brand color.
